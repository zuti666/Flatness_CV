id: C4
title: SAM adversarial direction matters; Gaussian random direction does not reproduce the effect
type: empirical
status: supported
---

Across all perturbation supports (factor, full, delta, all, frozen), the SAM direction consistently outperforms the corresponding Gaussian random direction by a large margin. The random variants are very close to SGD, ruling out generic noise injection as the explanation.

**Evidence**: Exp E (ImageNet-R r16 T20, SeqLoRA). For every support, Delta FAA(SAM − random) is +4.67 to +9.14. Random variants are within 0.5 FAA of SGD baseline.

**Implication for theory**: The effect is direction-specific (adversarial), not support-specific (noise injection). Supports the interpretation that Sh_Delta is the relevant quantity and that its adversarial direction matters.

**Remaining gap**: Does not by itself prove that raw factor-space is wrong — that requires rescaling control (Exp G).
