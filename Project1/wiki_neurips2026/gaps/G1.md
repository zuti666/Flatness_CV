The key theoretical claim is that raw LoRA factor-space sharpness (Sh_AB) is not the correct PAC-Bayesian object because it depends on the LoRA factorization, not on the effective model update ΔW = BA.

The same ΔW can be written as (A/c)(cB) for any c, so Sh_AB changes but the model output and Sh_Delta remain the same.

**Why it matters**: 
- sam_factor is currently the empirically strongest variant.
- Without the rescaling control, one could argue that raw factor-space is the right support.
- Exp G will confirm that the strong performance of sam_factor is because it approximately targets W_{Δ,t}, not because raw factor coordinates are intrinsically better.

**Addressing**: Exp G. See [experiments/exp_G.md](../experiments/exp_G.md).
