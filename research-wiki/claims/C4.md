# Claim

The superiority of adapter-subspace perturbation (AS^1) over full-space perturbation is robust across different LoRA organizational strategies: SeqLoRA (independent per-task adapters), IncLoRA (incrementally added adapters), and OLoRA (orthogonality-constrained adapters).

## Formal Statement

Robustness figure (Fig 6) and main results table show consistent AS^(1) > RS^(0) ordering across all three LoRA variants on ImageNet-C and ImageNet-P.

## Evidence

- Figure 6: Robustness curves for ImageNet-C/P across SeqLoRA / IncLoRA / OLoRA.
- Consistent with Lemma 4.2: the KL reduction holds for all three because each case maintains W_{Δ,t} as a fixed-dimensional subspace at each task.
