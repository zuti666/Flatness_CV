# Claim

Adapter-only first-order sharpness-aware minimization (AS^1) consistently improves continual learning performance over SGD and over full-parameter perturbation methods (RS^0 / Flat-LoRA approach) across all three LoRA organizational strategies in PECL.

## Formal Statement

Across SeqLoRA, IncLoRA, and OLoRA on ImageNet-C/R/P benchmarks: AS^(1) > AS^(0) > RS^(0) > SGD in terms of ΔAcc^cls (continual accuracy metric).

## Evidence

- **Table 2 (Scope effect)**: LoRA-scope perturbation consistently improves (+0.05 to +0.86 ΔAcc^cls); full-scope consistently degrades (−2.00 to −6.84 ΔAcc^cls).
- **Main results table**: AS^(1) ranking confirmed across three LoRA variants on ImageNet-C/R/P.
- **Additional planned**: ViT-B/16 on CUB200, Cars196, CIFAR100 + LLM+GAM (in preparation as of rebuttal, 2026-03).

## Status

**Supported** on current benchmark (ViT-B/16 + ImageNet-C/R/P). Reviewer concern (mBoF-W1, TSrA-C4): breadth limited to one architecture/dataset family. Additional experiments planned.
