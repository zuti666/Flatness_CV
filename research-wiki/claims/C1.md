# Claim

Under the frozen-backbone constraint in PECL, both the KL complexity term and the sharpness-dependent empirical term in the PAC-Bayes generalization bound reduce exactly to quantities defined on the LoRA update subspace W_{Δ,t}. Full-parameter quantities are irrelevant to the bound.

## Formal Statement

**Lemma 4.2 (KL reduction)**: KL(Q_t || P_t) = KL(Q_t^Δ || P_t^Δ) because the frozen parameters contribute a degenerate (zero-variance) factor to both Q_t and P_t, and KL is translation-invariant under fixed shifts.

**Lemma 4.3 + Theorem 4.4**: The sharpness term also reduces to adapter-subspace curvature via the frozen-backbone decomposition W_t = W_froz + ΔW_t.

## Evidence

- **Theoretical**: Proven in Theorem 4.4 of the paper. Key proof ingredient: supermartingale/DV technique at both model and hyper levels. Proof in Appendix B.1 (lines 2205–2374 of LaTeX source).
- **Supporting IncLoRA/OLoRA case**: For each task t, W_{Δ,t} = span of CURRENT task's (A_t, B_t) only — fixed dimension. Both P_t^Δ and Q_t^Δ are Gaussians on the same W_{Δ,t}, so Q_t^Δ ≪ P_t^Δ holds.

## Status

**Supported** — proven theorem in the paper. Accepted by reviewer ZRjU (score 5). Questioned by TSrA on novelty grounds, but the reduction itself is not disputed.
