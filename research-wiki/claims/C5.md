# Claim

The advantage of adapter-only subspace perturbation over full-space perturbation diminishes as LoRA rank increases, because higher rank adapters cover more of the parameter space and the distinction between "adapter subspace" and "full space" becomes less meaningful.

## Formal Statement

Ablation study (Figure 7 / rank ablation): the AS^(1) vs. RS^(0) performance gap decreases monotonically as rank increases toward full-rank fine-tuning.
