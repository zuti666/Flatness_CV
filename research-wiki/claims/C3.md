# Claim

Full-parameter perturbation (as advocated by Flat-LoRA) causes systematic performance degradation in frozen-backbone PECL settings, contrary to Flat-LoRA's recommendation.

## Formal Statement

Full-scope perturbation produces −2.00 to −6.84 ΔAcc^cls degradation across all three LoRA variants (SeqLoRA, IncLoRA, OLoRA) on the PECL benchmark.

## Evidence

- **Table 2**: Full-scope perturbation rows consistently negative across all conditions.
- **Mechanism**: Full-space perturbations introduce variations in frozen parameters outside the permissible update subspace W_{Δ,t}, creating a scope mismatch that accelerates performance degradation.

## Status

**Supported** — strong empirical evidence on current benchmarks. This directly contradicts Flat-LoRA's published recommendation and is the central empirical finding of the paper.
