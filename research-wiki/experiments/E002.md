# Experiment Description

Robustness check across three LoRA organizational strategies and ablation studies on rank and task length boundary conditions.

## Setup

- **Backbone**: ViT-B/16 pretrained on ImageNet-21K.
- **Datasets**: ImageNet-C, ImageNet-P (robustness curves), ImageNet-R.
- **LoRA variants**: SeqLoRA, IncLoRA, OLoRA.
- **Ablation factors**: LoRA rank (r ∈ {4, 8, 16, 32, 64}), task length (5, 10, 20 tasks).
- **Methods compared**: AS^(1), RS^(0), SGD.

## Key Results

- **LoRA organization robustness**: AS^(1) > RS^(0) holds across all three organizational strategies (Figure 6 robustness curves).
- **Rank ablation**: AS^(1) vs RS^(0) gap decreases as rank increases — consistent with theoretical prediction P4.
- **Task length**: Benefit holds across varying task lengths.
