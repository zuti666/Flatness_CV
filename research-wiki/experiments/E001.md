# Experiment Description

Systematic comparison of perturbation scope in frozen-backbone PECL:
- **AS^(0)**: Adapter-only zeroth-order (random) perturbation.
- **AS^(1)**: Adapter-only first-order (gradient-norm) perturbation.
- **RS^(0)**: Full-space random perturbation (Flat-LoRA approach).
- **SGD**: Baseline with no flatness regularization.

## Setup

- **Backbone**: ViT-B/16 pretrained on ImageNet-21K.
- **Datasets**: ImageNet-C (corruption robustness), ImageNet-R (rendition), ImageNet-P (perturbation).
- **LoRA variants**: SeqLoRA, IncLoRA, OLoRA.
- **Metric**: ΔAcc^cls (continual accuracy change relative to SGD baseline).
- **Incremental setting**: Inc-20 (20 classes per task).

## Key Results

- **AS^(1) > AS^(0) > RS^(0) > SGD** — consistent across all three LoRA variants.
- **AS^(1) scope benefit**: +0.05 to +0.86 ΔAcc^cls improvement over SGD.
- **RS^(0) harm**: −2.00 to −6.84 ΔAcc^cls degradation vs SGD (full-scope perturbation consistently hurts).
- Loss landscape visualization (3D): SAM optimizer finds flatter basin with lower loss than SGD.
