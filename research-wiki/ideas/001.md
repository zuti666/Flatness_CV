# Core Idea

In Pre-trained Encoder Continual Learning (PECL) with a frozen backbone, the theoretically relevant notion of sharpness is task-conditioned and supported on the admissible LoRA update subspace W_{Δ,t} — not on the full ambient parameter space. Consequently, adapter-only sharpness-aware minimization (AS^1) is both sufficient for generalization and superior to full-parameter perturbation (RS^0 / Flat-LoRA approach).

## Motivation

Active debate between two peer-reviewed positions:
- **LoRA-SAM**: Adapter-only perturbation provides effective implicit regularization.
- **Flat-LoRA (ICML 2025)**: Full-parameter perturbation is necessary for meaningful flatness.

This contradiction is unresolved theoretically. Our idea: derive a principled PAC-Bayes theory specifically for the frozen-backbone PECL setting to resolve this debate.

## Key Claims

- claim:C1 — Under frozen backbone, the PAC-Bayes bound reduces exactly to adapter-subspace quantities (KL and sharpness both on W_{Δ,t}).
- claim:C2 — Adapter-only perturbation (AS^1) consistently outperforms full-parameter perturbation in PECL experiments.
- claim:C3 — Full-parameter perturbation causes systematic performance degradation in PECL (contradicts Flat-LoRA).
- claim:C4 — The bound holds for SeqLoRA, IncLoRA, and OLoRA organizations.
- claim:C5 — The effect weakens as LoRA rank increases (boundary condition).

## Novel Technical Contributions

1. Sequential hierarchical PAC-Bayes with **time-varying hyperposterior** (classical P&L assumes fixed hyper-distribution).
2. **Explicit drift penalty** quantifying cross-task interference.
3. **Frozen-backbone support reduction**: KL(Q_t||P_t) = KL(Q_t^Δ||P_t^Δ) under frozen W_t^{froz}.
4. Use of supermartingale/Donsker-Varadhan technique (removes log(m) term vs. McAllester).

## Testable Predictions

- P1: AS perturbations outperform full-space perturbations in PECL.
- P2: Effect holds across SeqLoRA / IncLoRA / OLoRA.
- P3: AS^(1) > AS^(0) in fixed adapter scope (first-order > zeroth-order).
- P4: Distinction weakens at larger rank or when backbone partially unfreezes.
